Abstract
The Farmers' Market Price & Sales Tracker is a digital platform designed to help farmers manage and understand their agricultural sales more efficiently. The system focuses on the following key areas:
• Market Price Tracking: Records and displays current and historical prices of agricultural products, helping farmers compare prices and identify useful market trends.
• Sales Record Management: Allows farmers to maintain organized records of crops sold, quantities, selling prices, dates, and buyers, reducing dependence on manual paperwork.
• Revenue Monitoring: Calculates and summarizes sales and earnings so farmers can easily understand their financial performance for different crops and time periods.
• Market Comparison: Helps users compare prices and sales information across available markets, supporting better decisions about where and when to sell produce.
• Reports and Insights: Provides clear summaries of price changes, sales activity, and revenue, making important information easier to understand and use for future planning.
Overall, the proposed system brings market-price information and sales records into one organized platform. It can reduce manual record-keeping, improve access to important agricultural data, and support more informed, efficient, and data-driven marketing decisions for farmers.
